Introducing Orbis IO

Fleet Intelligence for the Connected Vehicle Era

We're pleased to announce the formation of Orbis IO Ltd, a new company dedicated to building fleet intelligence software purpose-built for the connected vehicle era.

Orbis is a collaboration between three fleet and leasing industry veterans — Alan Carreras, Simon Homer, and Andy Jackson — with almost a century of combined experience building and operating vehicle leasing companies in the UK.

The Problem Orbis Solves

Today's fleet managers have more data available than at any point in the industry's history. Connected vehicles generate real-time data. EV charging networks publish session-level cost data. DVLA, MOT, VED, and HMRC records are all digitally accessible. Insurance, maintenance, and mileage data sits across multiple systems.

The problem is that none of it talks to each other.

The result is a familiar one: spreadsheets as the integration layer, reactive maintenance, EV charging reimbursement reconciled manually, and strategic decisions made on information that's already stale.

What Orbis Does

Orbis pulls connected vehicle data, charging data, DVLA and MOT records, maintenance history, insurance renewals, and cost data into a single operational picture. The platform surfaces what matters before costs compound — from predictive maintenance signals to EV battery health projections to HMRC charging cost reconciliation.

It's fleet intelligence in the truest sense: not more dashboards, but better decisions. The underlying approach is patent-pending — a reflection of how different Orbis is from anything currently available in the market.

Built by Fleet People, for Fleet People

The founding team behind Orbis isn't a technology startup that discovered fleet management. Between us, we've spent decades at the sharp end of vehicle leasing and fleet operations — building lease companies, managing fleets, and living with the limitations of the tools available.

Orbis is the platform we wish had existed throughout our careers.

"We've spent a combined century in this industry. We know where fleet management breaks — and it's almost always at the joins between systems. Orbis exists to eliminate those joins."

— Alan Carreras, Founder

Working with Early Partners

Orbis is currently working with forward-thinking leasing companies and fleet operators to shape the platform against real-world scenarios. If you manage a fleet or leasing portfolio and the problem we're describing sounds familiar, we'd welcome the conversation.
