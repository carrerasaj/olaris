## Executive Summary

Olaris targets a specific, high-value UK market segment: fleet operators, lease companies, OEMs, and fleet brokers. These audiences search for solutions to specific operational problems (excess mileage liability, driver behaviour, licence compliance, cost visibility, EV transition planning). Unlike generic fleet management software competitors, Olaris can own niche keyword territories with lower competition through deep technical content and specific use-case targeting.

**Strategy Focus:**
- Primary: Organic search for mid-market and enterprise fleet operators
- Secondary: Thought leadership and technical authority in fleet intelligence
- Tertiary: Local UK fleet management search (geographic qualifiers)

**Expected Timeline:** 6-12 months to see substantial ranking improvements. Initial improvements (rankings moving into top 50) visible within 8-12 weeks with consistent content publication.

### 4.4 Canonical URLs

**Canonical Rules:**
- Homepage: `https://olaris.co.uk/` (not `https://olaris.co.uk/index.html`)
- Blog post: `https://olaris.co.uk/blog/excess-mileage-management` (without trailing slash if not present)
- Never point canonical to another domain (unless you're syndicating content)
- Don't canonicalize to a parent page (e.g., don't canonical blog post to `/blog`)

- Ignore mobile optimization
- Overlook Core Web Vitals

- Focus on user intent and problem-solving
- Create unique, original content
- Update and refresh top-performing content
- Monitor rankings and traffic consistently
- Build genuine relationships for link building
- Test new strategies and measure results
- Stay updated on Google algorithm changes
- Prioritize quality over quantity
- Document your strategy and results
