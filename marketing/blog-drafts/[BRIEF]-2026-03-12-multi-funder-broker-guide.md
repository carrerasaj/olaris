## Key Points to Cover

1. **The multi-funder reality** — successful brokers need access to multiple funders (10+) to offer competitive rates and coverage. But 10+ funders = 10+ completely different ways of processing the same requirement.

2. **The process fragmentation problem** — different systems, different portals, different SLAs, different KPIs, different reporting formats. Your team needs to know how to navigate each one. Multiply that across every deal.

3. **Tribal knowledge risk** — when your best processor leaves, how much funder-specific knowledge walks out the door? Which funder needs the application in a specific format? Which one has a 48-hour SLA on proposals? Which one requires manual TOBA renewal?

4. **TOBA complexity** — Terms of Business Agreements with each funder have different terms, different renewal dates, different commission structures. Miss a renewal and you lose access to that funder.

5. **Client-side confusion** — the complexity spills over to clients. Different invoice formats, different T&Cs, different contact points depending on which funder ended up with their deal. The client doesn't care about your back-office complexity — they just want consistency.

6. **The single view solution** — what a unified platform looks like for brokers: one view of all vehicles regardless of funder, normalised data, automated compliance tracking, consistent client experience.
