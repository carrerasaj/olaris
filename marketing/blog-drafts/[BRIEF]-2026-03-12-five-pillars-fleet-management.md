## The 5 Pillars (Olaris Framework)

1. **Vehicle Lifecycle & Acquisition** — from procurement through disposal. Acquisition strategy (lease vs buy), contract management, replacement cycling, residual value management. Tie in: 18 acquisitions experience.

2. **Driver Safety & Behaviour** — monitoring, scoring, coaching. Not surveillance — culture. Tie in: /features/driver-behaviour.

3. **Compliance & Regulatory** — DVLA licence checking, MOT tracking, duty of care, O-licence requirements, insurance verification. Tie in: /features/dvla-compliance.

4. **Cost Control & Financial Visibility** — real cost per vehicle, TCO analysis, excess mileage management, fuel monitoring, budget vs actual. Tie in: /features/cost-tracking, /features/mileage-tracking.

5. **Sustainability & EV Transition** — carbon reporting, ESG compliance, EV readiness assessment, charge point infrastructure. Tie in: /features/ev-transition.
