Frame it as "what I wish I'd known when evaluating fleet management platforms" — the questions nobody asks, the features that sound good in demos but don't work in practice, and what actually matters at scale.

## Key Points to Cover

1. **The problem with most comparison guides** — written by people who've never managed a fleet. They compare feature lists, not operational reality.

2. **Multi-manufacturer support is non-negotiable** — if your platform can't pull data from every vehicle in your fleet regardless of manufacturer, you're building another silo. Olaris connects to 15+.

3. **Mileage vs contract tracking** — most platforms track mileage. Very few automatically compare it against contract terms and alert you before excess charges hit.

4. **Integration depth matters more than feature count** — a platform with 50 features that don't talk to each other is worse than 10 that do.

5. **Compliance automation** — DVLA checking, driver licence verification, MOT tracking. If it's manual, it'll fall through the cracks at scale.

6. **Cost visibility** — real cost per vehicle, not just fuel + maintenance. Include insurance, depreciation, admin overhead, compliance costs.

7. **EV readiness** — any platform you buy today needs to handle mixed ICE/EV fleets. If it doesn't, you'll be replacing it within 2 years.

8. **The questions to ask in a demo** — practical checklist for fleet managers evaluating software.
