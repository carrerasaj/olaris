## Key Points to Cover

1. **Definition (snippet zone — first 2-3 sentences):**
"Fleet intelligence is the practice of aggregating real-time data from connected vehicles, telematics systems, and operational records into a unified platform that enables fleet operators to make data-driven decisions about cost, compliance, safety, and sustainability. Unlike traditional fleet management which focuses on administration and logistics, fleet intelligence transforms raw vehicle data into actionable insights."

2. **Fleet management vs fleet intelligence** — management is reactive (tracking, scheduling, recording). Intelligence is proactive (predicting excess mileage before it happens, identifying cost trends, flagging compliance gaps before they become fines).

3. **The data sources** — OEM telematics from 15+ manufacturers, DVLA, fuel cards, insurance, maintenance records, contract terms. Intelligence comes from connecting all of these, not just one.

4. **Why now?** — connected vehicles generate unprecedented data. The problem isn't data collection, it's making sense of it. Fleet intelligence platforms turn noise into signal.

5. **What fleet intelligence enables** — specific examples: predicting lease return costs 6 months early, identifying drivers trending toward risk before an incident, spotting EV conversion candidates based on actual usage patterns.

6. **The Olaris approach** — built by operators who lived the problem. Not a telematics company adding software, but a fleet company building technology.
