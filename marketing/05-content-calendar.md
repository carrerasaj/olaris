### How to Turn Google Alerts into Content (Quick Process)

**Step 1: Scan Daily (5–10 minutes)**
- Review incoming Google Alerts emails each morning
- Flag 1–2 items that align with your content pillars or current themes
- Assess newsworthiness and relevance to your audience

**Step 2: Contextualise (10–15 minutes)**
- Read the article/source
- Identify the "so what?" for your audience
- Connect to Olaris positioning or strategic themes

**Step 3: Draft Quick Post (15–20 minutes)**
- Write 180–230 word LinkedIn post (personal)
- Open with the headline/story
- Add 1–2 sentences of context or perspective
- Close with a CTA (link to blog post, question, or statement)
- Tag relevant contacts or companies (light touch)

**Step 4: Post & Monitor (5 minutes + ongoing)**
- Post on Monday, Wednesday, or Friday (when reactive insights are timely)
- Respond to first 5–10 comments within 2 hours
- Monitor for 24–48 hours; reply to substantive comments

**Step 5: Archive & Repurpose (Optional)**
- Save high-performing reactive posts
- Link them to related blog posts in future content calendars
- Use as data for quarterly review (which alert topics resonated most?)

- **Friday Post (Alan):** Weekly roundup post (curated 3–4 news items from alerts + commentary) — this is your primary reactive slot
- **Thursday Post (Company):** Usually planned, but can flex if alert triggers important company-level response

### Performance Tracking
- Tag reactive posts in your analytics (e.g., "Reactive" or "From Alerts") so you can measure:

  - Which alert topics generate most engagement?
  - Which alert keywords should you expand?

### Evergreen Best Practices
1. **Always respond to comments** within 24 hours (especially first 10)
2. **Link back consistently** — each LinkedIn post should drive somewhere (blog, newsletter, resource)
3. **Use CTAs everywhere** — don't leave readers guessing what to do next
4. **Date everything** — blog posts, LinkedIn posts, newsletter. Timeliness builds trust
5. **Monitor Google Alerts daily** — reactive content is highest engagement opportunity
6. **Batch content creation** — write 2–3 blog posts at once; draft 2 weeks' worth of LinkedIn posts in advance
7. **Test & adjust** — if a topic or format resonates, repeat it next month
8. **Share learnings** — quarterly reviews should inform strategy, not just document what happened
